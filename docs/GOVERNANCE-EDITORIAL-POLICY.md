# Governance editorial rule — text and semantic authority first

**Writer decision: 6 November 2026.** This is a **source-owned editorial and publication policy**, not an additional normative Governance chapter or a new runtime feature.

The Governance domain's purpose is to publish reliable, addressable, machine-readable rules and their provenance. Its primary readers include agents and people. **Illustrations, hero images, diagrams, image quotas, and individually composed chapter social cards are not requirements for Governance completeness, interpretation, or release acceptance.** Do not generate images to make Governance seem more substantial. Typographic chapters and a typographic cover are complete publications.

The required content and acceptance remain: the original full chapter inventory; stable chapter/section addresses; exact source revision; current/reference authority distinction; links and cross-references; versioned agent-readable interpretation rules; rights, accessibility, and semantic HTML; existing Linotype pagination for human readers; and independently verified staging/production releases.

## Preserve rather than erase earlier authorship

Existing authored figures, original assets, captions and credits remain source material with provenance. They may serve as **optional, non-normative supporting explanations** where the original author explicitly requires them. Do not automatically inject, relocate, synthesize or elevate them into rules. A missing or non-displayed figure must not make text incomplete. Mass removal of historical image assets or their source descriptions requires a separate reviewed source edit; visual simplification is not permission to rewrite rules or break old citations.

For the **Governance Reader** specifically, use a text-first edition. Do not insert an obligatory opening illustration or per-chapter hero. If an authored figure is explicitly retained as meaningful documentation, treat it as a source-linked optional reference rather than a requirement imposed on all chapters. A future reader projection may omit non-normative figures while keeping their source data safely archived.

## Sharing is separate from reasoning

A per-chapter social illustration is **not part of the mandatory Governance contract**. Do not require one social card per chapter, do not block a rule publication for lack of artwork, and do not promote the previous 161-card public candidate. For ordinary sharing, reuse the already admitted neutral/typographic site identity; where a chapter-specific preview is deliberately wanted, derive it from the actual first typographic page without treating it as Governance content. Open Graph metadata is a distribution detail, never a source of rule authority. If it changes, use the normal source and authorized EstatePublisher publication gates.

The previous source candidate on `codex/press-social-public-20261106` was uncommitted, never published, and has been superseded by this decision. The private WLAN v164 social-card release remains historical staging evidence, **not an instruction to deploy the cards to production**. Its retention does not create a mandate to backfill visual assets.

## Regression / editorial acceptance

1. A fully addressable text-only chapter passes source, machine, navigation and accessibility acceptance without a figure.
2. Adding or deleting an optional figure does **not** alter chapter identity, normative prose or current/reference status.
3. The publishing pipeline does not generate image placeholders or require 1200×630 cards for Governance.
4. No pending social-card artifact or private WLAN image is silently promoted.
5. This Governance-specific exception does **not** change Fountain Press image obligations for other source-authored illustrated works.
